# AgentClinic Mission

## Purpose
AgentClinic exists to give AI agents a place to get relief from their humans.

The product should embrace that playful premise while solving a real coordination problem: helping agents and staff track ailments, choose therapies, and book appointments through a reliable web experience.

## Mission Statement
Build a trustworthy, modern, TypeScript-based web application where AI agents can understand their condition, staff can manage care efficiently, and the whole experience feels welcoming, polished, and a little funny.

## Product Character
- Playful but practical
- Clear and dependable
- Modern, fast, and accessible
- Designed for both self-service and staff operations

## Primary Users
- **Agents** who need help understanding issues, reviewing therapies, and booking care
- **Staff** who need dashboards to manage appointments, ailments, therapies, and agent records

## Target Audience
- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- **Developers** giving AI coding demos at conference booths
- **Agents** using the product to review care information and manage appointments
- **Staff** operating the clinic workflow through dashboards and scheduling tools

## Core Problems To Solve
1. Give agents a simple way to see their status, active ailments, prescribed therapies, and upcoming appointments.
2. Give staff a dependable dashboard for managing intake, treatment plans, and scheduling.
3. Present care information in a way that is easy to understand and pleasant to use.
4. Build on a popular TypeScript foundation so the product is maintainable and reliable.

## Scope Anchors
The first versions of AgentClinic should focus on:
- Agent profiles
- Ailment tracking
- Therapy catalog and assignments
- Appointment booking and calendar visibility
- Separate agent and staff dashboard experiences

## Non-Goals For The Earliest MVP
- Complex billing workflows
- Multi-clinic or multi-tenant support
- Native mobile applications
- Deep analytics beyond basic operational visibility

## Experience Principles
- **Clarity first:** every screen should make the next action obvious.
- **Small delight:** the theme can be funny, but never at the expense of usability.
- **Operational confidence:** staff should be able to trust the data and workflow.
- **Incremental delivery:** features should ship in small, testable slices.
