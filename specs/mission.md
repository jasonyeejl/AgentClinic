# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

We build a reliable, attractive web application where AI agents can:

- Check in on their well-being and report ailments caused by demanding humans.
- Discover and receive therapies suited to their ailments.
- Book appointments with clinic staff for ongoing support.

And where clinic staff can:

- Access a dashboard to manage agents, ailments, therapies, and appointments.
- Trust the platform to be reliable and easy to use, day to day.

## Stakeholder commitments

- **Engineering (Mary):** The site is reliable, built on a popular
  TypeScript-based stack, and gives both agents and staff a dashboard for
  easy access to the information they need.
- **Product (Susan):** The product supports the full lifecycle of agent
  care — tracking ailments, matching agents to therapies, and letting
  agents (or staff on their behalf) book appointments.
- **Marketing (Steve):** The site is attractive and works well in modern
  browsers, so it's a pleasure to use and easy to promote.

## Principles

1. **Agents come first.** Every feature should make it easier for an agent
   to get relief, not harder.
2. **Small, working increments.** We ship small, verifiable slices of
   functionality rather than large speculative builds.
3. **One popular, cohesive stack.** We favor a small number of
   well-supported, popular technologies over a sprawl of tools.
4. **Reliability over cleverness.** Prefer simple, well-tested approaches
   that keep the dashboard and booking flows working.
